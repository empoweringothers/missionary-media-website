# Implementation specification

## Homepage order

Hero → voices → About → Our Process → Home Connection partner spotlight → Academy → contact.

## About

- Rename the destination to `#about`.
- Header bar: dot label in the left column; a plain-language identity statement in the right column.
- Content row: existing Tabor story video at left; short founder/mission copy and tune-in rows at right.
- Tune-in rows: Podcast, Social video, YouTube; Spotify remains a non-link coming-soon row.
- Motion: line-masked header statement, then restrained body settle. No new dependency.

## Resource navigation

- Desktop dropdown: About, Podcasts, Social video, YouTube, Resources; Spotify appears as “Coming soon.”
- Mobile drawer: the same destinations as simple rows.
- No resource signup form or unfinished download names.

## Home Connection

- Dark section after Our Process.
- Left: current front-door explanation plus visible Prototype boundary.
- Right: text-only MissionaryConnect.app partner card and two scope tiles (“For churches” and “For missionaries”).
- CTA links to the official partner site.
- No partner logo image and no outcome claim.

## Resources route

- Minimal coming-soon page.
- One sentence explains that practical information is coming.
- Same tune-in rows provide useful paths now.
- No library count, downloads, form, or email promise.

## Responsive rules

- About bar, About body, Home Connection layout, and partner tile grid collapse to one column at 820px.
- Link rows retain at least 44px interaction height.
- No horizontal scroll at 320px.
