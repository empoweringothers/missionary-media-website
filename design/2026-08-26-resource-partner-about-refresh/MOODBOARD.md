# Direction board — Editorial connection

## Visual anchors

1. **Editorial About bar** — dot label on the left, one confident sentence on the right, wide breathing room.
2. **Owned story media** — Tabor’s existing video card supplies the human proof; no stock media.
3. **Tune-in rows** — thin rules, channel name, plain state or arrow; patterned after the TaborNorm About information hierarchy.
4. **Home Connection partner field** — dark Missionary Media band with a pale partner card and two supporting tiles.
5. **Quiet Resources endpoint** — one headline, one sentence, and the same tune-in rows; no catalog theater.

## Brand translation

- Eloqwnt’s cool-gray field becomes Missionary Media cream.
- Eloqwnt’s black type stays Missionary Media ink, with blue/sky used only for state and action.
- Rounded founder portraits become the existing Tabor story video and a structured partner card.
- The partner block borrows Float UI’s left-copy/right-grid skeleton, not its logos or Tailwind implementation.

## Motion tone

- One scroll-triggered line rise for the About statement.
- A restrained body settle already present in the site.
- Small row hover/focus translations only; no marquee, auto-rotation, or perpetual logo animation.
