# Concepts and direction decision

## Concept A — Directory bar

About becomes a compact horizontal directory: identity statement at left and four tune-in links at right. Home Connection is a small logo-style strip below Our Process.

Tradeoff: fastest scan, but it compresses the founder story and makes the confirmed partnership look like a generic sponsor logo.

## Concept B — Editorial connection

About uses the measured Eloqwnt hierarchy: dot label left, large Missionary Media statement right, then Tabor’s story media and tune-in rows. Home Connection follows Our Process as a dark, proof-first partner feature using the Float UI copy-left/grid-right skeleton.

Tradeoff: longer than A, but it gives About, current focus, and partner disclosure distinct jobs without inventing content.

## Concept C — Media hub

About leads into a large card gallery for podcasts, short video, Spotify, and future resources; Home Connection becomes another card in the gallery.

Tradeoff: visually rich, but most channels do not yet have enough verified inventory. It recreates the premature library problem the user asked to remove.

## Selected direction

**Concept B — Editorial connection.** Selected autonomously from the user’s explicit priorities: it most faithfully uses the Eloqwnt reference, gives the confirmed partner meaningful context, preserves the existing story, and lets Resources become genuinely simple.
