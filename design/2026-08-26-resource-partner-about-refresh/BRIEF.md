# Missionary Media homepage and Resources simplification brief

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: implementation brief

## Required outcomes

- Preserve the approved four-step Our Process section.
- Replace “Who It’s For” with a real About destination and remove the Questions section.
- Turn Resources into a simple tune-in path: About, podcasts, social video, Spotify, and a quiet coming-soon page.
- Add Home Connection to the homepage and visibly disclose MissionaryConnect.app as its partner resource.
- Use the Eloqwnt About page as a measured layout-and-motion reference for the About bar.
- Use a license-cleared component path discovered through 21st.dev for the partner layout; do not invent a partner cloud.

## Audience and job

The primary reader is a missionary who already has a digital or media task in front of them. The page should help them understand who Missionary Media is, see the current Home Connection focus, and choose a simple place to keep listening or watching.

## Constraints

- Canonical deploy source is `public/`; legacy root HTML stays untouched.
- Home Connection is still prototype/proof-first. Do not publish ROI or performance claims.
- Only MissionaryConnect.app is confirmed for this partner treatment. No Adobe, Planning Center, ProPresenter, or placeholder logos.
- No unverified Spotify URL. Show Spotify as coming soon until the official destination is known.
- No Kit signup or unfinished resource names on the Resources page.
- No commit, push, or deploy in this task.

## Success test

At desktop and mobile widths, a reader can answer: who is Missionary Media, what is Home Connection, who is the disclosed partner, where can I tune in now, and what is still coming?
