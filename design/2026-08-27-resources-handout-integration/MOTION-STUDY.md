# Motion Study

## Posture

Quiet editorial motion. The handout should feel placed on a desk, not launched as a software product.

## Behaviors

- Reuse the site's existing `data-settle="rise"` entry behavior for the feature copy, cover, and lower shelves.
- Cover hover/focus: a small upward translation and shadow refinement only.
- Dropdown feature link: background/color transition consistent with current dropdown cells.
- No scroll-scrubbing, carousel, auto-rotation, or new JavaScript animation.

## Reduced motion

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, remove cover translation and dropdown/list padding shifts. All content remains visible with no animation dependency.
