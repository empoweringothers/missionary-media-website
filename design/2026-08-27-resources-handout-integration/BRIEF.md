# Resources Handout Integration Brief

## Job

Turn the existing Resources route from an information-coming page into a useful first-stop library anchored by the official `Digital Outreach Handout`.

## Audience

Missionaries and ministry workers who know digital outreach matters but need a practical starting point, not a broad creator-tech directory.

## Desired response

1. Understand what the handout helps with.
2. Open or download the four-page PDF.
3. Continue into a curated education or tool link only when it matches the next task.
4. Optionally follow Missionary Media through podcast or short video.

## Content hierarchy

1. Featured handout and its cover.
2. What is inside the guide.
3. Education and tools named in the guide.
4. `Tune in` / `Follow the work as it grows.`

## Navigation correction

The homepage Resources dropdown keeps its approved `Tune in` and `Follow the work as it grows.` framing, but removes `About` and generic `YouTube`. It points only to the featured guide, the education/tool shelf, podcast, social video, and Spotify status.

## Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS; no new dependency or build step.
- Reuse the current Missionary Media typography, colors, dropdown mechanics, and motion system.
- Use only the project-owned handout cover and PDF as new media.
- Label the handout truthfully as four pages; do not imply the unresolved one-page version exists.
- External education and tool links are optional starting points, not endorsements, requirements, or promised outcomes.
- Keep publication approval-gated.

## Success evidence

- The featured PDF can be opened and downloaded.
- The Resources page makes sense before a visitor knows Missionary Media.
- About and YouTube are absent from the Resources dropdown and Resources-page tune-in list.
- Desktop and narrow layouts have no broken assets, overflow, or clipped text.
