## Direction

**Selected mood:** field manual inside a calm course interface.

- Navy frame for authority and continuity with the Academy hero.
- Cream and white working surfaces instead of glossy floating cards.
- Cobalt for the active lesson, coral only for the capstone/live-review signal.
- Thin rules, numbered modules, and plain task labels.
- No people, stock photography, 3D boxes, glassmorphism, badges, or simulated social proof.
