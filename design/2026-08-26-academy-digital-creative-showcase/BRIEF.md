# Brief — Academy Digital Creative course showcase

## Problem

The Academy preview currently asks generic imagery and a ten-interview count to prove that a course exists. The imagery does not reveal a lesson, task, check, download, or review flow, and Home Connection is incorrectly presented as a course beside Digital Creative and Church IT.

## Audience and task

- A missionary or ministry helper should be able to see what a Digital Creative lesson would ask them to learn and finish.
- A ministry leader or advisor should be able to see that the curriculum has a sequence, measurable lesson parts, and a capstone.
- A visitor looking for help now should understand that Home Connection is one-on-one guidance, not a self-paced Academy course.

## Required message

Digital Creative is the first detailed Academy course plan. It uses short teaching blocks and immediate practice to help a learner finish one usable ministry artifact. Home Connection remains the live one-on-one path. Church IT is a planned course area, not a certification program.

## Design constraints

- Replace generic course packaging and lifestyle thumbnails with one original, deterministic interface illustration.
- Put a plain description beside the illustration; do not make the image carry every explanation alone.
- Use exact curriculum language. No placeholder counts, percentages, student outcomes, completion claims, enrollment state, badges, or certification implications.
- Keep the existing Missionary Media navy, cream, blue, coral, typography, curve, and spacing system.
- Keep the page useful without animation and readable at 320px.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Two learning rules are visible: one small teaching move and one real finished action.
2. The course plan quantifies seven modules, 5–10 minute teaching blocks, one guided task per lesson, and one live capstone review.
3. A complete sample lesson names its objective, practice task, knowledge check, download, live review, and capstone contribution.
4. Home Connection appears only as one-on-one/live guidance.
5. The interface preview is original project-owned SVG artwork with no external media or component dependency.
