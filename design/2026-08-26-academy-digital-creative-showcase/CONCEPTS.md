# Concepts — Academy Digital Creative course showcase

## Criteria

1. Shows a real lesson and the whole course path.
2. Makes the teaching-practice-check-download rhythm obvious.
3. Feels original and intentional inside the Missionary Media brand.
4. Scales to mobile without hiding meaning.
5. Does not imply an operating learning platform.

## Concept A — Course shelf

- Thesis: Replace three course photos with a row of detailed course covers and a curriculum table.
- Audience effect: Quickly communicates multiple topics.
- Supporting references: R03, R06.
- Structure/composition: Cover row above a module table.
- Type and color posture: Large editorial titles and cream cards.
- Imagery/material: Original diagrams on each cover.
- Motion posture: None.
- What it rejects: Generic photography.
- Risks and unknowns: Still emphasizes packaging and breadth; does not prove what one lesson feels like.
- Evaluation: Strong on scanning, weak on course-interior evidence.

## Concept B — Lesson operating screen

- Thesis: Show one original interface image containing the course map and a complete Digital Creative lesson, with a written anatomy beside it.
- Audience effect: Makes the course feel designed because the visitor can inspect the actual learning loop.
- Supporting references: R01, R02, R03, R04, R05, R06.
- Structure/composition: Large 16:10 interface illustration; module rail on the left; active lesson, objective, practice, check, download, review, and capstone in the workspace; explanatory copy beside the image on the page.
- Type and color posture: Compact UI typography, navy frame, cream work surface, cobalt active state, restrained coral review cue.
- Imagery/material: Project-owned vector interface only.
- Motion posture: Static; page scroll only.
- Reusable candidates: None.
- What it rejects: Course boxes, avatars, fake progress, completion badges, founder thumbnails.
- Risks and unknowns: Dense text must be tested at mobile sizes; the page copy must repeat the key details that become small inside the image.
- Evaluation: Best balance of course evidence, honesty, brand fit, and implementation safety.

## Concept C — Printed syllabus spread

- Thesis: Present Digital Creative like a carefully typeset academic syllabus with module cards, time budgets, and capstone rubric.
- Audience effect: Signals seriousness and deliberate curriculum planning.
- Supporting references: R03, R04, R06.
- Structure/composition: Two-page editorial spread with course map on one page and sample lesson on the other.
- Type and color posture: Paper-forward, large serif section numbers, minimal blue.
- Imagery/material: Printed-document simulation.
- Motion posture: None.
- What it rejects: Dashboard conventions.
- Risks and unknowns: Feels like a document rather than the planned learning experience; less direct for visitors expecting to see inside the course.
- Evaluation: Strong on rigor, weaker on product experience.

## Selection

Concept B is selected. It answers the user’s request for a detailed image of the course interior, gives every visible word a job, and lets the page explain the image without pretending that the underlying platform already exists.
