Why won’t it work together?

Before we talk

Frequently
asked questions.

01

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Explain what you’re trying to do in your own words. You don’t need to know the technical terms.

02

Can I call on behalf of a missionary?

Yes. A church, college, mission board, or supporter can help arrange support for a missionary. We’ll include the missionary in deciding what is needed.

03

What if I need a specialist?

If the work needs a specialist, I’ll help you look for someone who fits and explain what to ask before you hire them.

04

What if our ministry needs to stay private?

We’ll talk about your sharing limits before choosing tools or planning an update. Some questions need someone who knows the security needs of your field.

Let’s talk about what you need.

Tell me what you’re working on and where you need help. We’ll use the first 30 minutes to understand the problem and discuss how I can help.

The conversation is free. Any paid work starts only after you agree to the work and price.
