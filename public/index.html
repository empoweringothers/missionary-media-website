Questions

Know what to expect before you begin.

01What can I bring to coaching?

Bring a draft prayer letter, rough notes, photos, a voice memo, a phone-video idea, a gear or software question, a workflow, or a handoff you want to simplify.

02How long is the call, and what does it cost?

The first call is 30 minutes and it is free. If ongoing coaching makes sense after that, we will tell you what it costs before you commit to anything.

03Can I keep using my current tools?

Yes. Coaching begins with the tools you already use. If another tool would help, we’ll make the reason and tradeoff clear before you decide.

04What happens after the call?

You leave with one written next step you can act on that week. There is no follow-up sequence and no obligation. If you want another call, you book one.

05I serve somewhere sensitive. What can I share?

Please leave out field names, locations, and security details — we can work on the workflow without them. We will not hand you security advice we have not had validated by someone qualified to give it.

06What help is available now?

One-on-one digital and media coaching, and monthly live training. Practical resources are shared as they are verified and ready.
