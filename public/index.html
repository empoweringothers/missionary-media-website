The Story

Two missionaries. Same calling. Different access to help.

One had a guide and the patience to grow. The other got tangled, lost momentum, and went back to what he knew. Most missionaries we talk to are closer to the second.

Josh & Julie Mead · Senegal

He borrowed what works in the secular world — and the fruit came with time.

Josh applied the same practices that move people online to the field. It was not instant. He prayed for support, learned in public, and kept showing up. Years later, another missionary calls him "the cleanest example" of ministry-first media.

Calling + the right system + someone to walk it out.

Pastor Wilder · Guatemala

He started in tech with the right instinct — then got tangled and quietly walked away.

Wilder began building digital tools for his church. No guide. No way to tell if it was working. He defaulted back to what he knew — pulpit, visits, paper letters. Faithful work. Just not the reach his community needs.

Confusion without a guide costs missionaries their best digital efforts.

FAQ

Common questions from the mission field.

Short answers to what missionaries ask before they bring us the thing that is stuck.

I'm on slow wifi or phone-only. Can this still work?

Yes. Most coaching runs over voice or async messages. We send notes; you respond when you can. The tools we recommend run on a phone.

I'm in a restricted-access country. Is this safe?

Yes. We do not publish your face, name, or location without permission. We help you decide what is public, coded, delayed, or left unsaid.

I'm on deputation / first-term / veteran — which fits me?

All three. Deputation needs prayer cards, a clean presentation, and donor tracking. First-term needs a small rhythm before silence becomes normal. Veterans need to unify what is already scattered across Word, paper, Facebook, and church portals.

How much time per week does this take?

Whatever fits. A monthly thirty-minute call is enough for most missionaries. Heavier seasons — launch, furlough, deputation push — might be weekly for a stretch.

What does it cost? Can a sponsor cover it?

The discovery call is free. After that, pricing depends on scope — coaching, on-site training, IT setup. Sponsors fund missionary coaching directly, so the missionary often pays nothing.

I already use Mailchimp / a portal / something else. Do I have to switch?

No. We do not replace working tools. We fix what is drifting and add only what is missing.
