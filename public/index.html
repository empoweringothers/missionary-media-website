On the field

The world they walk.

This is the experience, not a method. The field is already full. The update becomes a second job. They skip it or wait. Home goes quiet.

  1. The week

    Ministry already fills the days. Digital work has to fit inside that, not on top of it.

  2. The extra work

    Staying close to the people back home takes time they do not have left.

  3. The pause

    The letter, the video, the tool choice sits. Not because they do not care.

  4. What home hears

    The people who sent them hear less, then less again.

Prototype · Home Connection

Church-side awareness. Missionary-side experience.

Two seats. MissionaryConnect is the proven church-side product. Missionary Media sits on the missionary-side experience.

Church side · MissionaryConnect

MissionaryConnect is the proven church-side product for awareness and display. It gives churches one place to organize and show the missionaries they support. Interactive maps and profiles can be shown on a lobby touchscreen, a television, or any modern web browser.

Missionaries can create or claim their own profile and keep its information current. Church members can then explore where they serve, learn more about their ministry, and see how to pray for them.

Missionary side · Missionary Media

Missionary Media sits with the missionary on the experience: the gap between what works digitally and what works on the field. We partner with MissionaryConnect. We do not replace it.
