How it works

Bring the left column. Leave with the right one.

  • 01

    Five churches want five different formats

    One wants the paper letter. One never wants to see a letter again. One wants it uploaded to their own portal. So you send everything five ways, by hand.

    What we do

    Ask your supporters once, then collapse the answers into two lanes — one short video and one printable page. You send twice, and the choice is locked for a year.

  • 02

    The letter eats the whole week

    Writing it is the part you can actually do. It is the laying out, resizing, exporting and attaching that turns one update into three evenings.

    What we do

    Separate the writing from the packaging, then template the packaging. One missionary put writing at 30–40% of the job — the other 60–70% is the part that should never be done twice.

  • 03

    You are sending them one at a time

    Copy, paste, next name. Or a mail tool you set up once, could not make work, and quietly stopped opening.

    What we do

    Pick one tool that fits the list you actually have, import it together, and send the first one while you are still on the call.

  • 04

    You have no idea if anyone reads it

    It goes out, nobody replies, and you cannot tell the difference between a supporter who is praying and one who has moved on.

    What we do

    Turn on the two numbers worth watching — who opened it and who wrote back — and add one line to the update that gives people something easy to reply to.

  • 05

    You do not know where to start

    The most repeated sentence in our interviews. Usually it means there are nine things and no order to them.

    What we do

    Name the one that matters this week, write it down, and set the rest aside on purpose so it stops taking up room.

Questions

Know what to expect before you begin.

01What can I bring to coaching?

Bring a draft prayer letter, rough notes, photos, a voice memo, a phone-video idea, a gear or software question, a workflow, or a handoff you want to simplify.

02How long is the call, and what does it cost?

The first call is 30 minutes and it is free. If ongoing coaching makes sense after that, we will tell you what it costs before you commit to anything.

03Can I keep using my current tools?

Yes. Coaching begins with the tools you already use. If another tool would help, we’ll make the reason and tradeoff clear before you decide.

04What happens after the call?

You leave with one written next step you can act on that week. There is no follow-up sequence and no obligation. If you want another call, you book one.

05I serve somewhere sensitive. What can I share?

Please leave out field names, locations, and security details — we can work on the workflow without them. We will not hand you security advice we have not had validated by someone qualified to give it.

06What help is available now?

One-on-one digital and media coaching, and monthly live training. Practical resources are shared as they are verified and ready.
