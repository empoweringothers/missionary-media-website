Before we talk

Frequently
asked questions.

01

What happens on the first call?

We’ll identify what you need and choose a practical next step. The call is a free 30-minute conversation. Scope and cost are explained before any paid work.

02

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. A cell phone with internet access is enough. After booking, you’ll receive an email with a link to the video call. Tap it at your appointment time, and I’ll help you from there.

03

Can I call on behalf of a missionary?

Yes. Tell me what your church or mission board is hearing from the missionary and what you’d like to make easier. Supporters and business leaders are welcome too.

04

Are there resources I can use now?

Yes. The free resources include the Digital Outreach Handout, learning links, and tools for ministry.

05

What if I need a specialist?

I’ll find a specialist for your question and connect you with them.
