Partner spotlight

We partner with MissionaryConnect to help close one of missions’ greatest communication divides: the distance between missionaries and the churches that support them.

MissionaryConnect gives churches one place to organize and display the missionaries they support. Its interactive maps and profiles can be shown on a lobby touchscreen, a television, or any modern web browser.

Missionaries can create or claim their own profile and keep its information current. Church members can then explore where they serve, learn more about their ministry, and see how to pray for them.
