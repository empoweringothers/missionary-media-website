Before we talk

Frequently
asked questions.

01

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Bring your question in your own words. You can choose a time from your phone or computer, and Calendly will show the meeting details before you confirm.

02

Can I call on behalf of a missionary?

Yes. Tell me what your church or mission board is hearing from the missionary and what you’d like to make easier. Supporters and business leaders are welcome too.

03

What if I need a specialist?

If the work needs a specialist, I’ll help you look for someone who fits and explain what to ask before you hire them.

What’s taking too much time?

Bring a technology problem or a task you keep putting off. We’ll spend 30 minutes talking through it and finding a practical next step.

The conversation is free. Any paid work starts only after you agree to the work and price.

Who is this call for? Required
Where would help be useful? Optional
