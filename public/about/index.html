About Tabor

How I help

I’m Tabor, founder of Missionary Media. I help missionaries choose and use technology so they have more time for ministry. I sit with you, listen to what you’re trying to do, and help you find a next step that fits your time and your team.

Background

I’ve helped missionaries in a few countries with audio and video, church systems, and digital security. My background includes visual arts, counseling, church media, and IT. That mix helps me work through both the tools and the people side of the work.
