Workspace & outreach

Google for Nonprofits

Google Workspace, Ad Grants, and other tools for eligible charitable organizations. Available products depend on your country.

Who applies

The registered organization. Schools and colleges should check Google for Education.

Check Google eligibility ↗🌐 Official eligibility guidelines

Software & hardware

TechSoup

Donated and discounted products for eligible organizations. Each provider sets its own requirements, and some offers have fees.

Who applies

Your organization. Check both TechSoup membership requirements and the individual product’s eligibility.

Explore TechSoup ↗🌐 Official eligibility guidance

Design & collaboration

Canva Nonprofits

Canva Pro features and collaboration tools for approved nonprofit teams.

Who applies

A registered charitable organization. Educational institutions are excluded from this program; Canva has separate education offerings.

🌐 Check Canva’s program ↗

Meetings & live teaching

Zoom for Nonprofits

Discounts on selected annual Zoom plans for qualifying nonprofit organizations.

Who applies

The organization, through Goodstack verification. Budget and account restrictions apply. Schools and colleges use Zoom’s education offerings.

🌐 Check Zoom’s program ↗
