Academy · Why these three come first

The first courses follow the work missionaries already carry.

In a coded set of 10 interviews, 7 people described content production that was too slow or hard to finish, 6 described fragmented supporter communication, and 4 described network, Wi-Fi, or basic IT problems. That is why this preview begins with these three course areas.

Research note: These interviews included missionaries and ministry leaders. Themes overlap, and the counts describe this interview set—not all missionaries.
