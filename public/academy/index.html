Developed & taught online

Digital Media

Learn to make photos, videos, and designs that communicate clearly.

Course topics+
  • PhotographyWork with light, framing, and the camera you have.
  • Video & audioPlan a short story, record usable pictures and sound, and edit the result.
  • DesignUse type, layout, and images to make readable graphics and book covers.
  • Putting it togetherPractice the skills in projects you can use in your ministry.

Developed & taught online

IT Basics

Understand your computer, connect your equipment, and work through everyday problems.

Course topics+
  • Computer hardwareParts, cables, ports, storage, printers, and building a PC.
  • Operating systemsBIOS, bootable USB drives, installation, updates, and recovery.
  • Networks & devicesWi-Fi, wired connections, laptops, and mobile devices.
  • Security & supportAccount protection, backups, troubleshooting, and documenting a fix.
  • Cloud & virtual computersUnderstand remote services and running a separate system on one computer.
