From my high-school classroom

Digital Media
for Ministry.

Explore video, photography, design, social media, marketing, and websites. Try each skill, find what interests you, and use what you learn in ministry.

10 weeks · Hands-on practice · A final project

Explore the ten-week class

Week 01

Introduction to Digital Media in Ministry

Understand the importance of digital media in ministry and get an overview of the course.

What we’ll cover

  • The role of digital media in modern ministry
  • An overview of digital media tools and techniques
  • Tabor’s personal story and course goals

In the classroom

  • Discuss how you already see digital media being used.
  • Share your interests and the areas you would like to explore.

Put it into practiceIdentify a digital media skill you would like to use in ministry.

Week 02

Basics of Video Production

Learn the fundamentals of video production for ministry.

What we’ll cover

  • Cameras, microphones and lighting
  • An introduction to iMovie and Adobe Premiere Pro

In the classroom

  • Set up and practice using video equipment.
  • Complete a basic video editing exercise.

Put it into practiceSet up a recording and make your first edit.

Week 03

Photography for Ministry

Develop basic photography skills for capturing meaningful images.

What we’ll cover

  • Cameras and lenses
  • Composition, lighting and editing techniques

In the classroom

  • Take and edit photos on a photography walk.
  • Review one another’s photos and discuss what works.

Put it into practiceCreate a small set of photos and improve them through feedback.

Week 04

Graphic Design Essentials

Learn basic graphic design principles and tools.

What we’ll cover

  • Canva and Adobe Photoshop
  • Color theory, typography and layout

In the classroom

  • Design a social media post or flyer.
  • Bring the design to a group critique and feedback session.

Put it into practiceMake a clear, readable post or flyer for a ministry activity.

Week 05

Social Media Strategies

Understand how to use social media for ministry outreach.

What we’ll cover

  • Popular social media platforms
  • Creating and managing social media content

In the classroom

  • Set up and improve a social media profile.
  • Develop a social media content calendar.

Put it into practicePlan what to share, where to share it, and when.

Week 06

Basics of Digital Marketing

Learn the fundamentals of digital marketing to promote ministry activities.

What we’ll cover

  • Digital marketing strategies
  • Email marketing
  • Search engine optimization: helping people find a page

In the classroom

  • Create a simple email campaign.
  • Practice improving a blog post or webpage for search.

Put it into practiceWrite an email and make a page easier for people to find.

Week 07

Website Development

Gain basic skills in website creation and management.

What we’ll cover

  • Website builders such as WordPress and Wix
  • Web design and user experience

In the classroom

  • Set up a basic website.
  • Add content and customize its design.

Put it into practiceBuild a simple page that helps a visitor understand the ministry.

Week 08

Integrated Digital Media Campaigns

Bring different digital media tools together in one coordinated campaign.

What we’ll cover

  • Planning and running a digital media campaign
  • Measuring the response and adjusting your approach

In the classroom

  • Develop a digital media campaign as a group.
  • Present the campaign and give one another feedback.

Put it into practiceConnect your writing, visuals and publishing plan around one ministry purpose.

Week 09

Ethical and Effective Communication

Understand ethical considerations and practice clear, responsible communication.

What we’ll cover

  • Ethical issues in digital media
  • Crisis communication and conflict management

In the classroom

  • Discuss scenarios involving ethical decisions.
  • Practice responding to a difficult situation through role-play.

Put it into practiceThink through how your words and images affect the people involved.

Week 10

Final Projects and Presentations

Present your work and reflect on the skills you have explored.

What we’ll cover

  • Final project presentations
  • The importance and possibilities of digital media in ministry

In the classroom

  • Present your final project to the class.
  • Discuss your next steps and personal commitments.

Put it into practiceShare what you made and choose how you want to keep using these skills.

This is the syllabus from my classroom course. The online version will build from this foundation.

Reading, tools & additional activities

Reading

From Social Media to Social Ministry by Nona Jones, alongside selected articles and ministry case studies.

Tools to try

Canva for design, Mailchimp for email, and WordPress or Wix for websites. Adobe Creative Suite is optional for video and photo editing.

Beyond the lesson

The syllabus also includes guest lectures, focused workshops such as livestreaming, and visits to churches or organizations when possible.
