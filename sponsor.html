For some missionaries, the difficulty is more than convenience. Transparency can carry real risk for visas, national believers, or the work itself. Silence carries a different cost: supporters drift, relationships fade, and the missionary feels the shame of going quiet. Missionary Media does not replace a sending organization's security decisions. It helps missionaries ask the right questions, protect sensitive details, and communicate with wisdom instead of fear.
