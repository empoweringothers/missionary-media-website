Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Can I give less than $67? +
Yes. Any amount helps, and you can give monthly or one-time. $67/month is simply the full cost of one missionary's care, so it's the most useful single gift. $134/month sponsors a missionary and helps fund the future builds.
Can my church sponsor a missionary? +
Absolutely, churches are the best fit. A church can sponsor one missionary or several. If you'd like to sponsor a group or talk through it, book a quick call.
Will I know who I'm helping? +
You'll get plain updates on what your giving funded and how it's helping missionaries on the field. For security reasons we don't expose names, faces, or locations of missionaries in sensitive regions.
Is my gift tax deductible? +
Not yet. Missionary Media is in the process of becoming a registered nonprofit. Until that's complete, gifts aren't tax-deductible, and we'll tell donors the day that changes.
What if sponsorships exceed current capacity? +
Gifts go toward missionary care in the order they come in. If giving outpaces what one founder can deliver one-on-one, it funds the next missionary and the systems that let us serve more without cutting corners.
